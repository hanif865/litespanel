How it works

  • Add your domain to Cloudflare and point its nameservers at Cloudflare (as Cloudflare instructs). Then a new subdomain resolves within seconds — no manual DNS.
  • Managed record types: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS. Other types on your Cloudflare zone are left alone.
  • Records the panel creates are tagged internally, so it will only ever update or delete its own records — never ones you added by hand in Cloudflare.
  • One token covers every zone in your Cloudflare account, so all your domains are managed together.
