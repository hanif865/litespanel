How it works

  • The panel database stays the source of truth for DNS. Whenever it writes a zone, it also mirrors the records to Cloudflare via the API.
  • Managed record types: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS. Other types on the Cloudflare zone are left alone.
  • Point your domain's nameservers at Cloudflare (as Cloudflare instructs on the dashboard). Then a new subdomain resolves within seconds — no manual DNS.
  • Records the panel creates are tagged internally, so it will only ever update or delete its own records — never ones you added by hand in Cloudflare.
